# Motion gaming

Experiences driven by whole-body movement, where the player's pose *is* the input.

| Sample | Renderer | AEP | Capabilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`rockdodge-android-tv`](rockdodge-android-tv/) | Android TV | 0.6.0 | `camera.pose@1`, `livevideo.person@1` |

A Unity build of RockDodge exists and will land here as `rockdodge-unity` once its plugin
assemblies are rebuilt against AEP 0.6.0. Two renderers of the same game, pinning the same
AEP, is the clearest demonstration the platform has that the canonical contract is real.
